Our Sundarban Story

From Jungle to Jar

Our honey comes from deep Sundarban mangrove forests, where local collectors risk life and limb to harvest honey from wild bee colonies hidden in the trees.

We partner with these communities to bring raw, unfiltered Sundarban honey directly to businesses—preserving taste, tradition, and sustainability.

WildGold is more than a honey brand; it’s a bridge between the Sundarban ecosystem and modern B2B buyers who value authenticity and ethics.
